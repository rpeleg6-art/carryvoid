Shipping & Returns

Clear expectations for TP Carryvoid orders.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Processing

Orders are processed after successful payment and supplier confirmation. Our current processing target is 2–3 business days. If an item cannot be confirmed, the order may be cancelled and refunded through the original payment method.

Shipping

Delivery time and availability vary by destination, carrier and product. For the currently checked US route, the supplier estimate is approximately 7–13 days after processing. Other destinations may take more or less time. Any delivery estimate is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival date.

Tracking & delivery

Where tracking is available, it is provided after shipment. Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address at checkout. Carrier delays, customs processing and local delivery conditions can affect final delivery time.

Returns

Eligible unused items may be requested for return within 30 days of delivery. Items should be unused, in resalable condition and, where applicable, in their original packaging. Return eligibility, return destination and any return shipping responsibility depend on the item, reason for return and applicable supplier terms. Please obtain return instructions before sending an item back.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

If an order arrives damaged, incorrect or incomplete, keep the packaging and transaction details and report the issue as soon as reasonably possible. Resolution may require photos or other order evidence.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory rights or remedies that apply to a customer under applicable consumer-protection law.

Product availability

Orders are subject to supplier confirmation and availability. Product details, colors and final production specifications may vary slightly by production batch or screen display.
