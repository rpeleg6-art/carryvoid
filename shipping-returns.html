Shipping & Returns

Clear expectations for TP Carryvoid orders.

Processing

Orders are processed after successful payment and supplier confirmation. The current processing target is 2–3 business days.

Shipping

Delivery time varies by destination. For the currently checked US route, the supplier estimate is 7–13 days after processing. Other destinations may differ.

Returns

Please contact TP Carryvoid before returning an item. Return eligibility depends on the item condition, the reason for return and supplier eligibility. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Product availability

Orders are subject to supplier confirmation and availability. Product details, colors and final production specifications may vary slightly by batch.
