Shipping & Returns

Processing

Orders are processed after successful payment and supplier confirmation. Target processing time is 2–3 business days.

Delivery

Delivery timing varies by destination. The currently checked US route is approximately 7–13 days after processing and is not guaranteed.

Tracking

Tracking information is provided when available from the shipping route.

Returns

Eligible unused items may be requested for return within 30 days of delivery. Items should be unused and in original condition unless mandatory consumer rights provide otherwise.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

Contact support promptly with order details and clear photos so the issue can be reviewed.
