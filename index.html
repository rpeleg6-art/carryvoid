Yes. The PayPal Hosted Button is a live checkout. Completing payment may charge real money.

No. Compression is intended to reduce volume and bulk; it does not reduce the actual weight of packed items.

Travel-case and luggage-style products are being designed around clear carrying solutions. The exact handle construction of each future SKU will be confirmed before that SKU becomes purchasable.
