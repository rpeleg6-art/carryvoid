## EPROLO operating notes
- Several candidate pages publicly state support for 7-day unconditional returns and shipment within 24 hours, but TP must not promise these timings unless the exact selected variant and destination route are verified at order time.
- EPROLO pages note that EU orders may incur additional tax; final checkout/customer copy must reflect the actual route and tax treatment.
- Public pages do not expose all landed costs reliably. Always use EPROLO's destination shipping calculator inside the account before converting a Coming Soon SKU to Buy Now.

## TP branding requirement
- ONE TP monogram only per item
- TP is the primary brand mark
- Carryvoid appears beneath TP in smaller type
- "Lose the air. Keep the trip." directly below Carryvoid
- Two collections:
  - Elegant & Dark: black, charcoal, navy, olive, sand
  - Colorful & Wild: electric blue, neon lime, sunset orange, berry pink, purple
- Request PMS/Pantone matching for production samples
- Request custom zipper pulls and one consistent valve design

## Sample/QC acceptance before scaling
- 30 compression cycles without zipper/valve failure
- 12-hour vacuum retention test
- Stitching and handles under loaded use
- Color rub / transfer test
- Pump electrical/battery documentation
- Packaging drop test
- Logo positioning confirmed once only
- Final measured dimensions and weights

## Commercial model
Phase 1: validate using supplier-direct fulfillment / low MOQ where available.
Phase 2: after sustained demand, negotiate branded production and private stock.
Phase 3: use US/EU fulfillment inventory only after economics support it.

## Supplier inquiry checklist
Ask every supplier for:
- Can you dropship one-by-one without MOQ?
- Can you apply TP branding per order? If not, minimum custom batch?
- Unit cost by SKU and quantity tier
- Freight cost to USA/UK/EU/Israel
- Actual tracked delivery times for the last 30 days
- Packaging and private-label MOQ
- Sample cost and sample lead time
- Battery certificates for electric pump
- Defect/replacement policy
- Production lead time
- DDP option and customs handling

Do not advertise exact compression percentages, waterproof ratings, airline approval, delivery guarantees or long warranties until the final sample and supplier documentation support them.
