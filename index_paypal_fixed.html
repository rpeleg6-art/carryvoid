FAQ

Simple answers.

How much space can TP products save?

Compression varies by fabric, product size and how full the bag is. We do not guarantee a fixed percentage until each final production SKU has been tested.

Can I cancel an order?

Cancellation requests are accepted before supplier processing. Because dropshipping orders move quickly, contact must be made as soon as possible; after dispatch, the returns policy applies.

What if my package is delayed?

Use the tracking number first. If tracking has not progressed for an extended period, contact support so the shipment can be investigated with the carrier/supplier.

Are duties and taxes included?

Not always. Duties, VAT or import charges depend on destination and may be collected locally unless checkout states otherwise.

What is covered by the 90-day defect policy?

Manufacturing faults such as failed seams, defective zippers, faulty valves or a defective included pump, subject to photo/video verification. Wear, misuse, punctures and airline damage are excluded.

When will my card be charged?

The site is currently connected to PayPal Sandbox for testing only. Sandbox payments do not charge real money. Live checkout will be enabled only after final verification.
