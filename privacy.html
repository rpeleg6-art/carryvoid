Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you browse or purchase from TP Carryvoid.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Information we may receive

When you place an order, payment and checkout providers may provide order information needed to process the transaction, such as your name, delivery details, purchased items, transaction status and relevant order identifiers. We do not intentionally collect or store full payment-card details on this website.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayPal. PayPal processes payment information under its own privacy terms and security practices. TP Carryvoid may receive transaction and order details needed to fulfill, support or refund an order.

How information is used

  • To process, fulfill and support orders.
  • To communicate about transaction, delivery, return or refund issues.
  • To prevent misuse, fraud or security problems.
  • To comply with legal, tax or accounting obligations where applicable.

Suppliers and service providers

Order information may be shared with suppliers, carriers, payment providers or other service providers only as reasonably necessary to process and deliver an order or resolve a customer-service issue.

Cookies and third-party technology

This site may load third-party technology required for checkout, including PayPal components. Those services may use cookies or similar technology according to their own policies. TP Carryvoid does not currently advertise a separate behavioral-advertising program on this site.

Data retention

Transaction and order information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for fulfillment, customer support, accounting, dispute handling, fraud prevention and legal compliance.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information. Mandatory privacy rights remain unaffected.
